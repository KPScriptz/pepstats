Connect your Riot account

PepStats reads your rank and match stats from your Riot account. Enter your Riot ID and paste a free Riot key — it stays on this PC and is never shared.

Get a free key at developer.riotgames.com → sign in with Riot → Register Product → Personal key. (It doesn't expire.)
